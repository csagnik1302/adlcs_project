Among the assassins -- accused, convicted or would-be -- are Jace Alexander as Lee Harvey Oswald, Victor Garber as John Wilkes Booth, Debra Monk as Sara Jane Moore, Greg Germann as John Hinckley, Annie Golden as Lynette " Squeaky " Fromme (who tried to kill President Ford) and Terrence Mann as Leon Czolgosz (who assassinated President McKinley).
