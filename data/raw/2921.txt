Committee-General Command of the Zapatist National Liberation Army " from the Mexican Southeast " on 15 March) (Text) 15 March 1994 To the people of Mexico, to the people and governments of the world, to the local and international media, brothers : The CCRI-CG (Indian Revolutionary Clandestine Committee-General Command) of the EZLN (Zapatist National Liberation Army) addresses all of you with respect and honor to state the following : Since the phase prior to the San Cristobal dialogue, the CCRI-CG of the EZLN requested legal support with various problems that the war process and the peace process presented for our Army.
