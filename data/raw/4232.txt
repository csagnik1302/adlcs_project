A Constitution Bench of the Supreme Court today initiated suo motu " contempt of court " proceedings against Acharya Giriraj Kishore, joint general secretary of the banned Vishwa Hindu Parishad (VHP), Mr. G.K. Mahajan, editor, printer and publisher of Kabardar India (Hindi weekly) and Mr. Ravi Thakur, reporter of the weekly for publication of certain statements reportedly made by Acharya Giriraj Kishore (on the Apex Court) in the edition of the Hindi weekly dated April 11 to 17.
